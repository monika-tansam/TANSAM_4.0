TANSAM COMPANY PROFILE & KNOWLEDGE BASE

ABOUT TANSAM
TANSAM stands for Tamil Nadu Smart and Advanced Manufacturing Center.
TANSAM is a Center of Excellence (CoE) powered by Siemens.
It was established as a Section 8 company under TIDCO (Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation) to drive industrial growth and serve as a global leader in smart manufacturing solutions.
TANSAM was inaugurated on 8 November 2022 by the Honourable Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, Thiru. M.K. Stalin.
Location: TIDEL Park, Rajiv Gandhi IT Expy, Tharamani, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600113.
TANSAM's goal is to help industries, MSMEs, startups, and academic institutions adopt future-ready Industry 4.0 technologies.

CORE COLLABORATION AREAS
- MSME Collaboration: Assisting micro, small, and medium enterprises in adopting digital twins and advanced tools.
- Start-Up Mentoring: Supporting entrepreneurs with industrial mentoring and resources.
- POCs & POVs: Creating Proof of Concepts and Proof of Values to validate technologies.
- Industry & Academic Spokes: Connecting educational institutions with industry partners.
- Joint Research & Development: Conducting collaborative R&D projects.
- Industry-Academic Collaboration: Providing certifications, courses, and internship projects to build careers.

THE NINE INNOVATION LABS
TANSAM features 9 state-of-the-art specialized labs powered by Siemens tools:
1. Product Innovation Lab: Uses Siemens tools to accelerate efficient, creative product development.
2. Predictive Engineering Lab: Uses Siemens tools to simulate, analyze, and optimize products before manufacturing.
3. Innovative Manufacturing Lab: Powers precision manufacturing through reverse engineering and 3D metal printing (Additive Manufacturing).
4. Smart Factory Lab: Uses Siemens Industry 4.0 tools to optimize, automate, and transform manufacturing floors.
5. Asset Performance Lab: Uses advanced engineering and computing methods to determine predictive and preventive asset performance.
6. Industrial IoT Lab: Uses edge computing and automation tools to enhance efficiency at the shop floor level.
7. Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) Center: Uses Siemens Teamcenter to streamline product lifecycles, collaboration, and innovation management.
8. AR | VR | XR Lab (Augmented / Virtual / Extended Reality): Develops immersive 3D simulations enhancing training, learning, and real-world industrial workflows.
9. Digital Technologies Center: Uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) techniques for vision computing and advanced computing needs.

TANSAM INTERNSHIP PROGRAM
TANSAM conducts high-quality, industry-oriented internship programs in Industry 4.0 technologies.
- Batches Completed: 4 batches have completed successfully.
- Participants: Students from engineering, arts, and science colleges across Tamil Nadu, other states of India, and from abroad.
- Training Style: Practical learning, hands-on training from experienced industry professionals. Students build mini-projects, collaborate in teams, and deliver final presentations.
- Key Technologies Covered:
  * Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning (ML)
  * Data Analytics
  * Full-Stack Development
  * AR/VR/XR (Augmented, Virtual & Extended Reality)
  * NX CAD (Computer-Aided Design)
  * Internet of Things (IoT)
  * PCB Design
  * Industrial Automation
  * Cloud Computing
  * Cybersecurity
- Career Impact: Feedback is overwhelmingly positive. Participants note a significant enhancement of technical knowledge, improved confidence, and valuable exposure to industry-grade software and practices.
