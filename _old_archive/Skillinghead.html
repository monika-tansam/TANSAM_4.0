What is TANSAM Skilling?

TANSAM (Tamil Nadu Smart and Advanced Manufacturing Centre), powered by Siemens, is the state’s premier Industry 4.0 skilling hub. It delivers hands-on training, consulting, and innovation labs to empower students, MSMEs, and professionals with future-ready skills in smart manufacturing, IoT, EV technology, AI, AR/VR, and cybersecurity.

Corporate

Industry 4.0 marks a transformative era in manufacturing and industrial practices, characterized by automation, real-time data, interconnected systems, and advanced technologies. To remain competitive, corporations must adapt by continuously enhancing the skills of their workforce.

Academia

TANSAM (Tamil Nadu Smart and Advanced Manufacturing Centre), powered by Siemens, partners with polytechnic colleges, engineering institutions, and universities across Tamil Nadu to deliver Industry 4.0 training, internships, and research labs. It is the academic outreach program of the Government of Tamil Nadu, designed to bridge the gap between classroom learning and industry needs

Read More

Naan Mudhalvan

Naan Mudhalvan is the flagship skill development program of the Government of Tamil Nadu, designed to empower students with Industry 4.0 skills including IoT, robotics, EV technology, additive manufacturing, AI, and cybersecurity. It bridges the gap between academic learning and industry needs, preparing youth for future‑ready careers in smart manufacturing, digital transformation, and emerging technologies
