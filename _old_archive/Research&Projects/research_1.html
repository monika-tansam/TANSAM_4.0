What is TANSAM AI?

TANSAM AI (Tamil Nadu Smart and Advanced Manufacturing Centre), powered by Siemens, is Tamil Nadu’s hub for Artificial Intelligence in Industry 4.0. It provides AI-driven training, solutions, and innovation labs to enable smarter manufacturing and digital transformation across Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, and Salem.

Why AI Matters for Tamil Nadu

  • • Government-backed initiative trusted by industries and institutions
  • • AI-powered smart factory solutions for predictive maintenance and quality control
  • • Supporting Chennai automotive, Coimbatore textile, Madurai academic, and Salem industrial clusters
  • • Future-ready AI careers with global demand
  • • Proven success with thousands of learners and institutions onboarded

Client: Roadways

Problem Statement / Project Scope:

  • Automate traffic classification & support road expansion planning.
  • Address limitations of manual vehicle counting & need for multi-vehicle classification accuracy.
  • AI-based system to automatically detect & classify various vehicle types using video analytics and sensor integration.

Solution Highlights:

  • High accuracy in real-time vehicle classification.
  • Seamless analysis of traffic data.
  • Improved traffic monitoring.

Value Addition: Near-accurate, real-time traffic data to support smarter road infrastructure planning and automated classification of vehicles on live feeds, reducing manual effort and improving consistency.

Client: Directorate of Public Health and Preventive Medicine

Problem Statement / Project Scope:

  • Mosquito breeding site identification & chlorine usage data collection is manual & decentralized.
  • No real-time geospatial monitoring or centralized data; limited field visibility & paper-based records.

Solution Highlights:

  • Cost-effective mobile platform integrating mosquito breeding & chlorine usage monitoring.
  • Implemented geotagging for accurate field data acquisition.
  • Responsive UX & interactive map-based selection for real-time data visibility.
  • Streamlined workflows, minimized manual app-switching, improved efficiency.

Value Addition: Faster, accurate field data capture, improved decision-making, and enhanced responsiveness to on-ground issues.

Client: Department of Public Health

Problem Statement / Project Scope:

  • Monitor drinking water quality based on chlorine concentration (ppm).
  • Detect over-chlorination and under-chlorination to ensure safe drinking water standards.
  • Enable digital data collection of chlorine values with real-time dashboard monitoring.

Solution Highlights:

  • AI model predicts chlorine ppm values from images captured via mobile app.
  • Real-time detection & classification of chlorine levels from field inputs.
  • Automatic alerts for ppm exceeding 2, enhancing public health safety.

Value Addition: Near-accurate classification of chlorine ppm values, automated analysis reducing manual errors, improved public health monitoring.

Client: Leather Industry

Problem Statement / Project Scope:

  • Automate quality inspection of shoes by measuring physical dimensions accurately.
  • Detect dimensional deviations to ensure consistent size, shape, and fit.
  • Utilize digital imaging and measurement tools to compare shoe dimensions against design specs.

Solution Highlights:

  • Automated detection & classification of dimensional deviations without manual tools.
  • Improved quality inspection, reduced human error, accelerated production checks.
  • Scalable solution enhancing manufacturing precision and rapid data-driven quality control.

Value Addition: Near-accurate measurement of shoe dimensions, automated analysis, reduced manual inspection errors, improved manufacturing precision and quality control efficiency.
