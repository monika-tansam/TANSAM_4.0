AR / VR Research Center

AR / VR Research Center

Client: Steel Industry

Problem Statement / Project Scope:

  • Objective: Enhance mechanical skill development through realistic and engaging virtual training modules.
  • Focus Areas: Immersive mechanical training, component-level interaction, error-free learning environment.
  • Approach: Build interactive 3D environment simulating mechanical assemblies with real-time guidance and feedback.
  • Outcome: Improved hands-on understanding, reduced training errors, and accelerated proficiency in mechanical procedures.

Solution Highlights:

  • Immersive Experience
  • Interactive Tools
  • Platform Accessibility
  • Learning Enhancement

Value Addition: Safe practice without risk of damaging real equipment, enhancing practical understanding of mechanical systems.

Client: Maritime Industry

Problem Statement / Project Scope:

  • Objective: Transform technical training through advanced holographic visualization and interactive learning experiences.
  • Focus Areas: Holographic training modules, immersive process visualization, enhanced technical skill development.
  • Approach: Design and deploy mixed reality environments projecting process workflows and system components in 3D space.
  • Outcome: Increased learner engagement, deeper process comprehension, and improved retention of complex technical procedures.

Solution Highlights:

  • Interactive 3D holographic simulations replicating ship processes with high spatial accuracy.
  • Hands-on procedural training through gesture-based interaction, guided steps, and contextual feedback.
  • Deployment on holographic displays & AR devices for intuitive, on-site or remote training.
  • Risk-free, repeatable practice to reinforce procedural knowledge and operational readiness.

Value Addition: Increased trainee involvement via immersive holographic interaction and hands-on learning without real-world risks.

Client: Healthcare

Problem Statement / Project Scope:

  • Objective: Enhance user understanding and engagement through dynamic digital experiences.
  • Focus Areas: Immersive visualization, interactive exploration, educational simulation.
  • Approach: Develop real-time 3D environments for experiential learning and system interaction.
  • Outcome: Improved knowledge retention, increased engagement, and accelerated training efficiency.

Solution Highlights:

  • Immersive Experience: 360-degree anatomical visualization with real-time interaction.
  • Interactive Tools: Controller-based exploration, layer peeling, system-specific highlighting.
  • Platform Accessibility: Deployed across VR headsets and desktops for seamless access.
  • Learning Enhancement: Multi-sensory learning to improve retention and comprehension.

Value Addition: Eliminated dependency on cadavers and physical specimens. Enabled self-paced hands-on learning, streamlined anatomy education, and improved comprehension speed and retention.

Client: Healthcare

Problem Statement / Project Scope:

  • Objective: Improve clinical skill acquisition through realistic, guided medical procedure simulations.
  • Focus Areas: Venipuncture simulation, step-by-step interactive training, safe repetitive practice.
  • Approach: Create 3D virtual environment replicating venipuncture process with real-time feedback and error correction.
  • Outcome: Increased learner confidence, reduced procedural errors, enhanced competency.

Solution Highlights:

  • Immersive Experience: Realistic 3D simulation of venipuncture with lifelike anatomical detail.
  • Interactive Tools: Guided training with step-by-step instructions and real-time feedback.
  • Platform Accessibility: Accessible on VR headsets and desktops for flexible training.
  • Learning Enhancement: Safe, repeatable practice to build confidence and reinforce procedural accuracy.

Value Addition: Reduced dependency on physical training kits and live demonstrations, enabling unlimited safe practice for learners.
