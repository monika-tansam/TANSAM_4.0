Industrial IOT & Edge Computing

Sensors and Edge devices deployed across the shopfloor enable legacy equipment and machinery systems to become “Smart”. Intelligence is added to these systems by collating data, using edge computing devices to locally or globally process this data, providing computational intelligence to drive informed decision-making. This leads to a host of benefits that include real-time monitoring & control, reduced latency, improved scalability, enhanced privacy, cost-efficiency, and accurate and faster decision making.

Using data, statistical algorithms, and machine learning techniques to analyze historical data and make predictions about future events or outcomes. It involves extracting valuable insights from past patterns and trends to forecast what might happen in the future.
