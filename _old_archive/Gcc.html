Global Career Connect

About Us

TANSAM – Global Career Connect (GCC), backed by Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation (TIDCO), Government of Tamil Nadu, is a trusted platform connecting skilled professionals with global career opportunities. We bridge the gap between talent and industries worldwide, offering tailored recruitment, skill development, and career advancement solutions.

Who We Are

Global Career Connect is a dynamic and forward-thinking organisation specialising in end-to-end recruitment and talent solutions across industries. With a strong global outlook and deep industry expertise, we help businesses access the right talent while empowering professionals to achieve their career aspirations.

Our Mission

To create a world where talent meets opportunity beyond boundaries, empowering dreams to transform into global success stories.
