About TANSAM

Tamil Nadu’s Industry 4.0 Hub Since 2022

TANSAM (Tamil Nadu Smart and Advanced Manufacturing), powered by Siemens, is the state’s of the arts Industry 4.0 Centre of Excellence. Located at Tidel Park, Chennai, TANSAM helps industries, MSMEs, and academic institutions adopt future‑ready technologies such as IoT, digital twins, AI, AR/VR, and smart factory solutions.

Inaugurated on 8 November 2022 by Honourable Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, Thiru. M.K. Stalin. Established as a Section 8 company under TIDCO to drive industrial growth,a global leader in smart manufacturing solutions.

STUDENT SUCCESS STORY

TANSAM Internship Program

Bridging Academia & Industry

TANSAM has been successfully conducting industry-oriented internship programs in a wide range of Industry 4.0 technologies, equipping students with practical skills that align with current and future industry requirements.

Students from engineering, arts, and science colleges across various districts of Tamil Nadu, as well as participants from other states in India and abroad, have actively taken part in these internship programs.

Practical Learning & Mentorship

Throughout the internship, students receive hands-on training from experienced industry professionals. They work on mini projects, gain practical exposure to real-world applications, collaborate with fellow participants, and develop innovative solutions using modern tools and technologies. At the end of the program, students present their projects, demonstrating the knowledge and skills they have acquired during the internship.

Technologies Covered

AI & Machine Learning (ML) Data Analytics Full-Stack Web Dev AR, VR & XR NX CAD Design Embedded & IoT PCB Design PLC & Industrial Automation Cloud Computing Cybersecurity & many more

We are happy to state that our collaboration with Renault Nissan has achieved a new flagship milestone.

An innovation done by our team has been recognized in the TPM Event (Total Productive Maintenance) organized by CII. Our team has won the Silver Medal. The system implemented provides a robust, low-cost, and highly accurate solution for quality control. By integrating AI, Computer Vision and PLC automation, significant failure modes in the engine assembly process, has been eliminated. The system provides real-time validation of operator movements. This solution addresses a critical quality risk in the engine assembly line.

Principal, Seshan from our team had participated in the event conducted this week along with few members from Renault Nissan.
