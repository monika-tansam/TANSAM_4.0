About Us

What is TANSAM?

The Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation (TIDCO) is establishing the Tamil Nadu Smart and Advanced Manufacturing Centre (TANSAM) at TIDEL Park, Chennai. As India’s first-of-its-kind Centre of Excellence (CoE), TANSAM provides a dedicated advanced manufacturing ecosystem to support Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) and students across Tamil Nadu.

👉 Partner with TANSAM – accelerate your Industry 4.0 journey

Driving Industry Growth

TANSAM is designed to strengthen key industries in Tamil Nadu through advanced manufacturing, automation, and digital transformation in domains such as:

  • ✔ Aerospace – Advanced design and simulation.
  • ✔ Defense – Cutting-edge manufacturing solutions.
  • ✔ Automotive – Smart factory automation and EV technology.
  • ✔ Electric Vehicles (EVs) – Innovation in sustainable mobility.

The Tamil Nadu Smart and Advanced Manufacturing Centre (TANSAM) provides SMBs, MSMEs, and students with complete access to the Siemens Xcelerator portfolio of solutions and services—enabling them to use advanced technologies that would otherwise be unaffordable. In its short existence, TANSAM has already accelerated industrial innovation and driven economic growth by introducing digitalization and automation capabilities across Tamil Nadu.

👉 Partner with TANSAM Academy – empower your workforce with Industry 4.0 skills.

Joint Initiative

TANSAM is a joint initiative between Siemens, the Government of Tamil Nadu, and TIDCO (Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation). Its vision is to:

  • ✔ Incubate innovative start-ups.
  • ✔ Provide Industry 4.0 training to engineering students.
  • ✔ Strengthen academia–industry collaboration through SPOKE centres across Tamil Nadu.

👉 Join Tamil Nadu’s first-of-its-kind Centre of Excellence – shaping the future of smart manufacturing.

Impact So Far

Since its grand opening in November 2022, inaugurated by Honourable Chief Minister Thiru. M. K. Stalin, TANSAM has achieved remarkable milestones:

  • ✔

    97,500+ students trained in Industry 4.0 technologies.

  • ✔

    700+ professors upskilled with Siemens tools.

  • ✔

    2,000+ SMBs supported with product development and digital solutions.

  • ✔

    Goal: 500,000 students trained in the upcoming year across Tamilnadu.
