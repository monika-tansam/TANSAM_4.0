Faculty Development Program:

TANSAM has trained over 1,700 faculty members, providing them with advanced skills and valuable industry experience. The 6-day faculty development program is thoughtfully designed to align academic teaching with current industry demands, thereby enhancing the overall quality of education.

The training program is structured with the first 4 days dedicated to classroom training, followed by 2 days focused on assessments that include written tests and practical evaluations to gauge participants’ technical understanding and application capabilities. Conducted in both online and offline modes depending on session type, the FDP ensured inclusive participation and provided faculty with digital learning materials and real-time project exposure. The initiative not only enhanced faculty readiness to deliver high-impact student training under the Naan Mudhalvan program but also fostered a collaborative learning environment that bridges the gap between academic instruction and industry requirements.

Following the successful completion of the faculty development program, the student development program will commence. This structured approach ensures that faculty are well-equipped with the knowledge and insights needed to effectively guide and support students throughout their training, fostering a more impactful and industry-ready learning experience.

Student Development Program:

Tamil Nadu Smart and Advanced Manufacturing (TANSAM), under the flagship Naan Mudhalvan scheme of the Government of Tamil Nadu, organized a 3-month Student Development Program (SDP) aimed at training students in advanced manufacturing and emerging technologies. This program targeted students from both engineering and polytechnic colleges across various zones in Tamil Nadu, with the goal of improving their technical capabilities and employability.

A 10-week program for Polytechnic students and a 12-week program for Engineering students. Assessments are conducted during the final week of the training to evaluate the students' learning progress. Throughout the program, students participate in activities such as hackathons and industrial visits to gain practical experience and industry exposure. Upon completion of the course, internships and placement drives are organized to help the trained students secure job opportunities and further develop their careers.
