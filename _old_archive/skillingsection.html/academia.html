The rapid evolution of Industry 4.0 demands a paradigm shift in academic curricula to prepare students for the jobs of tomorrow. Our academic skilling initiatives bridge the gap between traditional education and modern industry needs, integrating theory with hands-on experience to make students job-ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is TANSAM Academia free? Yes, it is a government-backed initiative for Tamil Nadu institutions.
  • Which colleges can join? Engineering, polytechnic, and universities across Tamil Nadu.
  • What facilities are provided? Labs, Siemens software, faculty training, internships, and placements.
  • How do we register? Contact TANSAM via tansam.org/academia or email contact@tansam.org.
  • Is it recognized by industries? Yes, leading Tamil Nadu companies actively hire students trained under TANSAM.

Key Components of Academic Skilling for Industry 4.0:

Curriculum Modernization:
  • Courses on AI, ML, and Deep Learning.
  • Modules on Big Data, Cloud, and Cybersecurity.
  • Subjects covering IoT and IIoT applications.
Hands-on Lab & Project-Based Learning:
  • Labs with robotics, automation systems, and 3D printers.
  • Projects simulating real-world industrial challenges.
  • Hackathons and innovation-based competitions.
Cross-Disciplinary Education:
  • Encouraging collaboration across disciplines.
  • Programs emphasizing IT and OT convergence.
  • Soft skills: creativity, adaptability, and problem-solving.
Faculty Development & Training:
  • Training educators on Industry 4.0 technologies.
  • Developing engaging and industry-relevant content.
Industry-Academia Collaboration:
  • Internships, mentorships, and partnerships with industries.
  • Guest lectures and collaborative projects.
  • Industry-recognized certification programs.
